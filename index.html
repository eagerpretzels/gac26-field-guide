Getting to the Gala by T

Red Line from Harvard to Park Street (about 10 min), then Green Line E branch to Prudential (about 12 min). Allow 35 to 40 minutes door to door.

Fare $2.40. Tap a contactless card or phone at the gate; no CharlieCard needed. Since 1 Sep 2026 transfers within two hours are free (pilot). A 7-day pass is $22.50.
